## Automatic progression and stops

Each workflow reserves the whole four-contact envelope first. Checking is
mandatory; a scored quality finding can use at most one repair with mandatory
recheck. A fully metered content rejection, including truncated/schema-invalid
output, remains failed and is bound into an immutable outcome receipt. It cannot
become accepted merely because its cost is settled. Later unrelated cases may
continue under the same approval, including after a failed development case.
This differs deliberately from the stopped historical staged policy.

Hard stops include producer/schema/source/settings/approval drift, changed old
receipts, accounting/outcome corruption, input fit refusal, exhausted caps or
budget, expiry/revocation, cancellation, provider errors and **any new uncertain
completion**. The campaign is stopped, remaining cases are unrun, liabilities
retained and no blind replay occurs. Duplicate invocation skips immutable
terminal outcomes; an unfinished identity is never replaced to evade the journal.
