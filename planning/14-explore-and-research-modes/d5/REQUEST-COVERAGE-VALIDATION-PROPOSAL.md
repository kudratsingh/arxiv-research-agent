## Acceptance and stop rules

Mechanically require exact selected handles, source/version/material/hash/parser/
Unicode offsets, local and transformed schema validity, all reciprocal claim/part
references, mandatory checker input digests, bounded calls and auditable accounting.
Never accept factual content solely because a quotation resolves. Checker reviews
whole-claim meaning and optional prose plus atomicity, qualifications, request
coverage and priority. One decisive first-check defect may use the existing single
repair; recheck is mandatory and residual failure rejects. Invalid/missing verdicts,
provenance, drift, provider errors, uncertain completion, exhausted limits or
admission refusal stop with no adaptive replay, alternate IDs or automatic retries.

Development success means a wire-valid, provenance-valid, explicitly scoped result
with honest request-part accounting and no unresolved supplied quality findings.
It does not prove scientific correctness, checker reliability or usefulness.
Lack of evidence may be a useful partial answer; it is not request completion.
No positive semantic label can override failed mechanical integrity.

At most two non-abstaining cases × six claims = **12 claims**. Therefore this scope
cannot meet the preserved20-item factual/checker minimum, nor establish error rates
or everyday quality. The all-abstention case contributes zero factual claims.
Existing thresholds and held-out denominators are unchanged; missing labels and
empty denominators remain HOLD/UNSCORED. Human fields stay blank. No retrospective
threshold tuning or machine-to-human label promotion.
