# Two evaluations for two purposes

A generally interesting paper and evidence for a specific question are different
targets. Report them separately even when the same paper and Reader are used.
This document sets evaluation design for future mode work, not new observed
results or a change to the current freeze.

| Layer | Measures | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Software | Isolation, exact versions/anchors, retries, conflicts, budgets, UI geometry, schema integrity | Factual quality, understanding or personal usefulness |
| Acquisition | Actual coverage windows, dates/provenance, duplicates, extraction quality by section | Complete literature coverage or reliable equations/tables from a few samples |
| Explore discovery | Owner useful/maybe/irrelevant, novelty/variety, relevance to interests, manageable effort | Answer quality for a specific task question |
| Learning | Owner comprehension, prerequisite usefulness, explanation correctness, optional explicit self-check | Mastery inferred from views or completion clicks |
| Research retrieval | Per-question known-reference recall, judged precision, conflicting evidence, unjudged fraction | Recall across the literature or final answer factuality |
| Research synthesis | Completion, atomic claims, exact provenance, whole-claim support, omissions/abstention, latency/cost | Human endorsement or checker statistical qualification from a tiny run |
| Continuity | Exact-passage return, saved-question resume, real owner-session experience | Longitudinal usefulness from automated same-day sessions |

## Retrieval comparison design

Freeze candidate pool, versioned questions and profile, filters, clock, date
policy, top-k, deterministic ties, formulas and proposed reference provenance
before inspecting a new comparison. Compare query-only, profile-only and combined
ranking with the same eligible pool. General interests cannot be an implicit
Research eligibility gate. Profile-only may repeat the same daily candidates;
question-reference recall is not an evaluation of their Explore usefulness.

Use per-question results and query-level paired intervals; correlated claims
are not independent samples. Empty denominators remain unscored. Label proposed
machine-assisted reference judgments and unjudged candidates; conditional
precision over a few judged items is not precision over the full result list.
Missing publication dates make chronological ranking a limited diagnostic;
never derive dates from identifiers or acquisition timestamps.

## Next validation set proposal

After the diagnostic report, select a new preregistered set reflecting the saved
profile: core agents/retrieval/evaluation weighted above control/spatial
exploration. Include question matches outside profile topics, genuine multi-source
comparisons, insufficient evidence and abstract-only limitations. Keep development
cases separate. Human reference/support labels stay blank until supplied; proposed
agent annotations remain explicitly machine-assisted.

Inspect atomicity and whether the selected passage supports the whole claim.
Record omitted relevant conditions, counterevidence and comparisons. Wrong
version, wrong quote/offset, unselected passage, ambiguous quote, truncated JSON
and extraction failures must never gain factual credit. Do not weaken support
checks or relabel unsupported facts as inference to improve a score.

Any later paid validation set requires **fresh explicit owner approval**, plus
its own sealed exact request/envelope and cumulative budget checks. The present
continuation is only q04–q10 and stops on any new failure; unused money is not
permission to add or repair-and-replay cases. Everyday generation separately
requires approval and quality evidence. Reserve mandatory checking/recheck capacity and
stop on new contract, accounting or admission failure. Unknown liability stays
conservative and distinct from rejected content.

## Owner review at a working milestone

Show a small real candidate packet with source, match reason, exact availability
and estimated effort. Leave useful/maybe/irrelevant and optional reason blank.
For reading ask separately about density, comprehension, implementation value
and resumption. Ask only for judgments the owner actually made. Automated sessions
exercise mechanics with their actual dates; they cannot stand in for five days
of owner use or prove real-time source freshness.
