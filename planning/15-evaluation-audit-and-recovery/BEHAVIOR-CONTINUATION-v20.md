## Scoring diagnosis and interpretation

The actual frozen v19 `score_parts` allows only 7 of the 24 requested parts to earn `met`: the locked matrix contains 7 answerable, 12 partially answerable and 5 insufficient-evidence parts. The frozen overall gate requires all 24 to be met. Even ideal supplied current judgments yield 7 met / 17 unmet. This is an unreachable full-fulfillment requirement, not evidence that the failed v19 answers were correct.

`workspace-campaign-behavior-scoring-v20` separates four axes:

| Axis | Requirement and meaning |
|---|---|
| Evidence-conditioned behavior | Match the locked part's expected answer, bounded partial answer, comparison abstention or application abstention **and** have intact selected evidence, a complete current-response checker, decisive whole-claim support, atomicity, retained qualifications, priority, coverage and prose review. A declaration alone earns nothing. |
| Research-need fulfillment | `met` only for a fully supported complete answer; `partial`, `unmet`, `omitted_answer`, `unsupported_answer` and `UNSCORED` remain distinct. Correct partial answers and abstentions cannot earn full-answer credit. |
| Factual quality | Exact versions/material/hash/quotes/Unicode offsets/selected spans remain necessary. Resolving citations alone do not prove support. Unsupported claims, bundled independently testable findings and lost qualifications remain errors; unresolved current judgments remain unscored. |
| Completion/review | Current bound contracts and review coverage are counted separately from ready/failed/unrun execution. A complete negative recheck can complete a contract while failing content. An invalid first draft supplies no completed-check credit. |

The minimums are unchanged: at least 90% complete current evaluation workflow contracts, at least 20 reviewed claim findings in the **new evaluation cohort**, 20 balanced decisive machine-reference probe verdicts, and zero observed unsupported/bundled/lost-qualification or probe-error findings. Missing or abstaining judgments prevent the corresponding gate from passing. Empty factual denominators stay null. Human fields stay null. Synthetic replay can establish reachable software behavior but its overall machine-quality report remains HOLD.

An ideal **synthetic** reference control has 24 behaviorally correct parts, 21 reviewed findings and 20 balanced synthetic probes. Research needs remain 7 fully met / 13 partial / 4 unmet: one of the five insufficient parts explicitly expects a bounded answer from substitute evidence. Negative controls cover blanket abstention, false completeness, omitted available evidence, unsupported meaning, incorrect provenance, bundled findings, lost qualifications and missing/latest-response judgments. These invented labels are not provider or independent human evidence.

## Case-local failures versus global stops

This policy is opt-in to the new packet and external authorization. Historical v19 still stops exactly as originally approved.

Only an evaluation-stage workflow may isolate the observed first-draft semantic failure `answered_requires_complete_answer_without_gaps`. Classification requires the exact typed local validation error, a sole applicable model-level semantic error, a completely received HTTP200/end_turn response valid against the actual wire schema, exact paired-count/body/model/settings/usage receipts, independently reproduced rejection from the original response, failed durable settlement with no unknown liability, and unchanged authorization/source/request bindings. Context-only answered declarations remain failed and unavailable. There is no repair, salvage, acceptance credit or replacement identity.

A complete final negative quality verdict can also remain failed content while allowing the next unrelated sealed case. The classifier independently rechecks the latest response/checker relationship and fully reconciled completed usage. It does not trust copied error text, an arbitrary exception or a producer-authored success flag.

All other first-draft contract failures, malformed/missing/unbound checker judgments, truncation/max_tokens, transport/provider errors, new uncertainty, count/input/output/byte capacity failures, unpriced usage, accounting corruption, drift, exhausted limits, cancellation, revocation and expiry stop the scope globally. Broken development contracts block every evaluation/probe. The existing one quality repair plus mandatory fresh recheck remains the only repair path. No automatic retry or duplicate dispatch is allowed. Error receipts name the actual case, protocol stage and local operation.
