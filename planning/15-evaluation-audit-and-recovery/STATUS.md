## W1 — current-response assessments

The production adapter and report share one canonical checker-context builder.
The context now contains the canonical whole-response digest and section-reference
layout in addition to current claims, evidence, coverage and rendered prose.
The report requires the exact completed checker after the latest draft/repair,
with matching model/settings/schema, preceding paired count, request hashes,
contact ordinals, raw response and validated step. Identical request bodies can
legitimately recur after an unchanged repair; journal order distinguishes them.

Coverage-only, prose-only, section-only, claim/evidence changes, pending repair,
uncertain recheck and malformed/duplicated/out-of-order bindings leave current
coverage, quality, prose and whole-claim judgments **unavailable**. Mechanical
provenance remains a separate result. Executor acceptance and the one-repair /
mandatory-recheck limit are unchanged.

The complete-journal predecessor also tried parsing a completed count receipt as
an earlier check. The audit's checker-only projection exposed the stale-judgment
path. Both reproductions are retained. The archived synthetic replay is immutable;
separately labelled successor fixtures rebuild prospective check requests and are
software evidence only. Old checker requests without the stronger response binding
remain historical; they are not silently attached to a new response or packet.

## W2 — prospective answerability

`reference/answerability-v1.json` contains machine-authored expectations for all
eight exposed-corpus evaluation cases. The successor packet binds each of the
24 exact requested parts to selected source versions, scope, hashes, parser,
Unicode offsets, quote digest, ranking and selection. Reference labels stay out
of producer/checker payloads; human labels remain null.

The report separates expected answerability, bound machine coverage, omitted
answers, unsupported answers, unmet needs and appropriate abstention. A faithful
comparison abstention can be appropriate while leaving a research request unmet.
Missing vehicle context requires full application abstention, including source
note parts. Abstract-only experiment outlines are bounded partial answers, not
reproduction protocols. Separate support/atomicity/qualification/prose results
still determine factual acceptability; quote identity alone proves none of them.

Prospective scoring policy: `workspace-campaign-scoring-v13`. Historical v7r2 3/8,
v8r4b and stopped development results and original scoring remain unchanged.
Eight evaluation cases, twenty generated factual claims and twenty checker probes
remain visible minimums; development facts never fill evaluation minimums.
Missing judgments stay UNSCORED/HOLD. Reference-consistent abstention is not a
completed answer; the overall diagnostic stays HOLD when research needs remain
unmet, without relabelling that abstention as an unsupported factual assertion.

New report derivatives carry an analysis receipt binding packet, raw-result digest,
scorer code, policy and locked matrix. They never overwrite raw responses or prior
reports. Current owner/human usefulness and qualification remain unmeasured.
