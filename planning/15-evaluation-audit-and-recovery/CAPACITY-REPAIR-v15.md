## Prose, logical cardinalities and protective bounds

500 characters. Individual factual claims also have an explicit 10,000-character
safety guard, consistent with grounding and current-response digest validation;
this generous bound does not excuse bundled findings or lost qualifications. Exceeding a target produces a formatting observation, not a failed
verdict. No explanation, quote, claim or JSON is shortened to pass. Reader disclosure
retains complete stored explanations and claims; display access does not establish
semantic support. The original 104/110-character checker reasons remain unchanged
in permanent evidence. A separately labelled successor fixture can reach semantic

for separate findings and their study scope, comparator, metric definition and

must each be answered, lack evidence, or be explicitly omitted. Context notes do
not answer a comparison. Omitted available information is distinct from absent
evidence. Full application abstention has no claims or claim references.

There are at most three separately retained citations per Learn claim or six per Research claim, twelve explicitly
selected coherent evidence units, three materials, eight requested parts and

## Complete workflow, routes and time

The mandatory path stays draft, check, at most one repair, then mandatory recheck.
A fifth Messages call, missing judgment, wrong digest, invalid reference, unsupported
assertion, unscored prose, missing qualification or incomplete/max_tokens response
cannot become a pass. Every current judgment must follow and bind the exact current
response, including coverage, section references and prose. A previous check is not
reused on an unrechecked repair.

The route stays Sonnet 5.5 with its existing medium generation settings and Opus
5.5 adaptive High for check/recheck/probes. Proposed output allowances are 8,000
and 12,000 tokens respectively, including thinking. Every actual Messages request
requires its own exact model/body/settings paired count at or below 32,000 tokens;
40,000 input tokens is the conservative monetary reservation, not usable context.
Mock byte counts never establish real token fit.

Count requests have 60 seconds, drafts/repairs 120, checks/rechecks 240, and the
whole workflow 1,200 seconds from its original durable reservation. Each stage is
limited by remaining workflow time. Schema compilation, thinking and output transfer
are inside the transport deadline. Restart cannot reset the origin, retry a
possibly dispatched contact, or release a hold. The fake executor's separate lease
is not a provider lease. Cancellation, expiry, timeout and uncertainty fences remain.

Support-only checker probes use their own exact support/prose schema and instructions.
Workflow checks additionally require atomicity, all four qualification dimensions,
request coverage and priority. A valid negative verdict is different from a broken
contract. Fully metered negative quality can be preserved while unrelated cases
