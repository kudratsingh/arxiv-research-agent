## One authoritative mapping

The generated format retains the simple actual-claims array and required keyed
coverage object. Coverage alone assigns answer and context claim IDs to each
sealed requested part. The model must not emit `comparison_claims` or
`comparison_basis`; both removed properties are rejected by the local model and
the actual SDK-transformed wire schema.

Claim IDs are assigned from array positions. For internal Reader compatibility,
comparative IDs are derived from answer references belonging to comparison
parts. This bookkeeping never proves relevance or factual support. Every such
comparative claim must itself cite selected passages from at least two distinct
works. Two unrelated single-source notes cannot satisfy that condition by being
pooled together.

| Intended response | Required basis | Coverage and presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualitative method comparison | An actual contrast supported by separately cited method evidence from the necessary sources | May answer a qualitative question without a shared benchmark; whole meaning and qualifications still checked |
| Empirical/performance comparison | A comparative finding supported by compatible tasks, comparators, metric definitions, conditions and evaluation evidence | A resolving citation does not establish compatibility or superiority |
| Partial comparison | Actual supported comparative findings and all genuine remaining gaps | Explicitly partial; no full-answer credit |
| Comparison abstention with source notes | Separately cited notes that establish source context, without answering the requested comparison | Comparison explicitly unanswered; context receives no answer credit |
| Missing application context | Application-sealed assessment lacks required context | Full abstention only, no claims or claim references; ask for the missing details |

The independent current-response checker assesses support, atomicity, retained
qualifications, request relevance/coverage, comparison meaning, priority and
prose. Declaring an answer and supplying two citations cannot substitute for
those judgments. Uncertain or missing assessments stay unavailable or on hold.
No semantic decision is inferred from a keyword or from quote resolution.

The checker sees the server-derived response disposition and also the original
structural wire body tag. This avoids presenting a comparison abstention as a
semantic answer simply because its claim-bearing wire branch is named `answer`.
The canonical response digest still binds every original generated field.
