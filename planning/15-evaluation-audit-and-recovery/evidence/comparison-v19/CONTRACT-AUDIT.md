# Constraint and contract audit

This maps the prospective v19 rules to their actual enforcement. Schema validity, current-response identity, semantic support and human qualification are separate conclusions. The wire deliberately uses flat status rows to preserve compiler headroom; its enum alone does not prove conditional validity.

| Rule | Wire representation | Local validation or server derivation | Semantic checker and remaining limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed output shape and selected handles | Closed objects, bounded arrays and selected-handle enums; SDK-transformed local/wire schemas are exported per identity. | Pydantic validators, byte/capacity limits and selected material/version/quote/offset checks reject malformed or out-of-scope output. Some SDK-transformed bounds remain local constraints rather than grammar branches. | Resolving a citation does not prove its whole claim, bibliographic provenance or scientific correctness. |
| Claim identity | Claims are emitted in order; wire claims do not supply their own IDs. | Server normalization assigns c1, c2 and so on for the current response. Whole current-response and checker input digests bind those positions. | A prior response's valid claim IDs or checks do not score a later repair. |
| Sole claim-to-request-part mapping | Coverage rows contain answer_claim_ids and context_claim_ids. There is no model-supplied comparison_claims or comparison_basis list. | Coverage is the authoritative mapping. Comparison metadata is derived internally from comparative answer links, never a second source of answer credit. | The checker judges whether each linked claim answers the original requested comparison. A server-derived list is not semantic credit. |
| Flat coverage status | A row supplies answered, partial, lacks_evidence or omitted, plus gap reasons and references. No separate per-status compiler branch is added for every part. | answered requires nonempty answers and only none as its gap; partial requires answers and genuine remaining gaps; lacks_evidence forbids answers and requires evidence-gap reasons; omitted forbids answers and requires omission reasons. Unknown, duplicate, orphaned or overlapping references and invalid priority behavior are rejected locally. | The checker independently judges each requested part and retains critiques. A locally consistent answered declaration can still be false or incomplete. Partial receives no full-answer credit. |
| Question roles | Context and factual-premise references are distinct fields. | Neutral intents forbid premise references; investigate_claim requires scored premise references. Unknown, overlapping or duplicate references fail locally. | Topic context asserts no factual premise. Whole factual premises and unsupported assertions outside scored claims still require review. |
| Comparative source bases | Each comparative answer is an actual claim with its own selected evidence handles. | Each comparative answer claim must itself cite at least two selected works. Pooling two single-source summary claims is insufficient. Source notes remain context when the requested comparison is unanswered. | Two resolving selected works are necessary, not sufficient. The checker must judge the comparative meaning and each source's support. |
| Qualitative versus empirical comparison | The v19 part description distinguishes qualitative method contrasts from performance conclusions. | The server preserves source scope, gaps and the original requested-part identity. It does not infer common benchmarks or superiority from two citations. | Supported qualitative method contrasts need no shared benchmark. Performance conclusions require compatible tasks, comparators, metrics, conditions and qualifications. Correct source facts do not justify falsely complete performance coverage. |
| Gap codes and displayed requests | Gap reason codes remain frozen enums. | v19 renders common_evaluation as “Request compatible evaluation tasks, comparators, metrics and conditions before drawing performance conclusions.” Historical v18 text is unchanged. | A request for compatible empirical evidence does not prohibit supported qualitative comparison. No unknown implementation or evaluation detail is invented. |
| Body marker and aggregate coverage | The claim-bearing wire body tag remains answer. | Request and comparison dispositions are derived from coverage. Checker response_layout uses that derived disposition while wire_body_tag preserves the original structural marker. The digest still binds every raw wire field. | Neither the body marker nor server-derived comparison metadata establishes full-answer credit or support. |
| Independent current-response review | Context-bound keyed checks require every claim, requested part and exact prose location. Empty finding lists are explicit judgments; no model-supplied global prose-clear flag exists. | The server normalizes the v18 consistency checker contract, derives aggregates, verifies exact locations and binds the completed checker after the latest draft or repair. | Whole-claim support, atomicity, qualifications, requested-part coverage and unsupported or uncertain prose are independently judged. Coverage critiques are not silently recategorized as unsupported factual prose. These machine judgments are not human qualification. |
| Repair and progression | At most one repair; any repair must receive a new check. | Production journals, ordinals and exact response/context digests prevent stale first-check reuse. Both development contracts must be complete before evaluation. | A valid negative quality verdict remains a failure and can permit an unrelated sealed identity. A broken development contract stops the schedule; a complete negative judgment cannot become accepted content. |
| Exact paired counts and SDK schemas | Fourteen initial bodies and their exact same-model count bodies are frozen. Future check/repair/recheck prompts depend on actual prior responses. | Each generation needs a fresh count on the exact body after removing only max_tokens and temperature. Future dynamic bodies are not manufactured, and neither historical nor mock counts may be reused for a new provider request. |

Eight exposed controls retain inherited 2610.* benchmark abstracts. Material identity is checked. | Evaluation bibliographic metadata and public-paper acquisition provenance were not newly verified. The frozen questions, ranking, selection, answerability and score thresholds are unchanged; this is not held-out evidence. |
| Authority and liabilities | Packet/proposal/template remain false; owner fields and approval expiry are unset. | The writer requires the actual verified packet, exact compiled bindings, current source and producer hashes, five-field public programme descriptor and live private ancestry checks. ## What the schema guards establish

Nine claim-bearing initial workflow schemas have one conservatively counted union parameter, zero optional parameters and one expanded union node. The application-context abstention initial schema and four fixed keyed probe schemas have zero unions and zero optionals. These are local measurements of the SDK-transformed schema shape; actual SDK body equality is separately proved by the same-packet replay receipts.

The schema guard checks documented ceilings and conservative planning headroom. It is not the remote compiler's internal grammar algorithm. One union and zero optionals do not establish remote acceptance, output completeness, token fit, research quality or checker reliability. The 44 actual SDK mock generation/count pairs, when the separate protected proof is present, establish protected software behavior using synthetic counts and judgments.

Historical v18's four remote-acceptance observations stay in their original evidence chain. They are compatibility observations for those earlier bodies and contracts. They are not new v19 count receipts or independent semantic/human qualification. This campaign adds no repeated standalone diagnostics: it proposes only the frozen 14 identities with their bounded workflow calls and four probe batches.
