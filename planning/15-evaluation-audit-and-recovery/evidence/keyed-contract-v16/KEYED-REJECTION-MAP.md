The actual SDK strips maxItems in some arrays. The final v16 checker therefore has neither a per-location nor an aggregate finding-count cap; full findings remain bounded by compact/normalized byte guards. Other local reference cardinalities and nonblank judgments protect required semantic relationships. Enums/required closed-map identities survive the actual transform. Serialized duplicate-key rejection runs before normalization; unsupported meaning, exact provenance, request priority and current-check causality remain necessary semantic checks.
