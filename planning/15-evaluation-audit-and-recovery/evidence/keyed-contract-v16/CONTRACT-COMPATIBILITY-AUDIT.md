## Narrow repair eligibility

A recoverable completed **first draft** must have validated usage and accounting, a fully received allowed model/end_turn body, strict duplicate-free finite object parsing, exact current sealed request/source/code bindings and no cancellation/expiry/revocation/uncertainty. Schema/semantic violations of the generated draft may become explicit diagnostic input to the existing single repair; they are never accepted or auto-corrected. Unknown handles, stale references, wrong dispositions, blank claims, invalid coverage or reference binding still fail original acceptance. The repair can change them only by producing a completely new valid response against unchanged sealed source selection, then passing a mandatory independently bound checker. Preserve all original error locations/codes. Scope/identity/source/producer/packet drift, transport errors, unpriced responses, truncation/max_tokens, duplicate/nonfinite serialization, checker-contract failure, late response, second draft/repair failure and spent repair allowance remain terminal. This prospective policy must be named and bound in packet/runtime; old failed records must not be reopened.
